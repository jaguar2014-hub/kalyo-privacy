Privacy Policy

Kalyo nutrition tracking app — Last updated: September 9, 2026

No tracking No server Your data stays on your device

Kalyo ("the App") is a nutrition tracking application developed by an independent developer. This page explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Data we do NOT collect

Kalyo does not collect, store on a server, or share with third parties any personal data about you. All your food logs, profile information, weight entries and activity records are stored exclusively on your device using local storage (AsyncStorage). No account creation is required to use the App.

2. Data that stays on your device only

The following data is stored locally and never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself:

  • Your profile (name, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal)
  • Your food diary (meals, foods logged, quantities, dates)
  • Your weight history and weight goals
  • Your activity log (type, duration, calories burned)
  • Your app preferences (language, theme)

You can delete all locally stored data at any time from the Settings screen ("Delete my data").

3. Optional: Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides us with your basic profile information (name, email, profile photo) to associate your local data with your Google identity. This data is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to any server operated by us. You can revoke this association at any time from the Settings screen.

We do not have access to your Google password, contacts, or any other Google account data.

4. Third-party services used by the App

The App queries the following external services to provide food and image recognition features. These queries are anonymous (no personal data is sent) and the responses are used only to display results:

  • Open Food Facts — food database by barcode (community-driven, public data)
  • CIQUAL / ANSES — French food composition database
  • USDA FoodData Central — US food composition database
  • A food image recognition service — used only when you tap the camera feature

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

6. Your rights

Since no personal data is stored on our servers, there is no data to retrieve, modify or delete on our end. All data lives on your device and you have full control over it.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL.

8. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, contact: jaguar2014@gmail.com
